# U11 Source/Build vs EZE4/U12 Stock Binary Audit — M2/M3

Date: 2026-08-23. Scope: binary and observable ABI comparison between a reproduced U11 build from OSRC `X510XXSBDZB4` and the stock firmware `X510XXUCEZE4`/U12. No image or module was flashed or edited.

## Verdict

- **NO-GO for physical write**: no proof of U11↔EZE4/U12 ABI equivalence.
- The U11 build is internally coherent, but its kernel is `5.15.180` while stock EZE4 exposes `5.15.189-android13-3-33478785`.
- The compiled base DTB and stock DTB are semantically different and incomplete as evidence; the three DTBO overlays are byte-for-byte identical.
- The absence of exact EZE4/U12 source prevents attributing observed differences with certainty to actual release changes, different toolchain, or absence of Samsung local patches.

## Kernel, vermagic, and dependencies — Facts

- U11 version: `5.15.180`; unique vermagic across 282 modules: `5.15.180 SMP preempt mod_unload modversions aarch64`.
- Observable version in stock kernel: `5.15.189-android13-3-33478785`.
- Embedded configuration present in both kernels. Relevant observable options match: `MODVERSIONS=y`, `MODULE_SIG=y`, `MODULE_SIG_PROTECT=y`, `MODULE_SIG_ALL=y`, SHA-1, `TRIM_UNUSED_KSYMS=y`, `CFI_CLANG=y`, `ARM64_MODULE_PLTS=y`, and `RELOCATABLE=y`.
- The full `.config` has 18 diff hunks, but automated extraction does not permit attributing all differences without validating the generation environment.
- U11 exports 10,365 symbols in `Module.symvers`; stock does not include a comparable `Module.symvers`. Stock modules are also not present as files in this inventory: only their `modules.load`, `modules.dep`, and `modules.softdep` metadata.
- Order comparison: 280 common modules in identical relative order; only stock has `sec_debug_test.ko`; only U11 has `a96t396.ko` and `input_booster_lkm.ko`.
- The U11 hard graph is closed: 282 entries and zero missing hard dependencies. Obsolete softdep references (`exynos_thermal`, `memory_group_manager`, `pcie_exynos_rc`, `s2dos05_regulator`) also exist in stock metadata.

**Observable ABI:** matching flags and relative order does not prove equality of modversions CRCs, type signatures, private structures, exported symbols, or behavior. With versions `5.15.180` and `5.15.189-android13-3-33478785`, cross-loading stock/U11 modules cannot be considered safe without CRC tables from both kernels, and those tables are not available here.

## DTB, DTBO, and reserved-memory — Facts

- The stock base DTB in `vendor_boot` and the r03/recovery DTB have identical hash: `4d6675ee...fea2820a`; size 239,588 B.
- The compiled U11 DTB measures 239,584 B and has a different hash. The binary difference includes length and content; there is no byte-for-byte equality.
- The semantic tool reports **DIFFERENT (INCOMPLETE)**: 1,493 nodes per side, 4 material differences, and 14 unresolved references (7 on each side).
- Material differences:
  1. `/ems/pe-list/list@0`: representation/value of `cpus` differs.
  2. `/ems/pe-list/list@1`: order of `cpus` ranges differs.
  3. `/mfc`: `debug_mode` is `1` in U11 and `0` in stock.
  4. `/scsc_wifibt@11B40000`: `cpu_table_rps` is `"00","  "` in U11 vs `"00","00","40","40"` in stock.
- Unresolved references on both sides include `camera_rmem`, five SysMMUs (`brp_s0`, `csis_s0`, `cstat_s0`, `rgbp_s0`, `yuvp_s0`), and `wlbt_hw_ver`.
- The full `reserved-memory` subtree has exactly the same number of non-empty lines (10,878) in both DTS files, but is **not** textually identical; the localized diff corresponds to the material differences already listed, not additional reserved nodes.
- The three overlays r00/r01/r04 are byte-for-byte identical between U11 and stock.

**Limited Interpretation:** the overlays are binary equivalents, but that does not extend equivalence to the base DTB or to `reserved-memory`. The incomplete status of the diff prevents asserting that reserved regions are functionally equal even if their textual inventory matches except for the four cited properties.

## Source/build vs EZE4/U12 — Facts and Inferences

Facts:

- The available OSRC is `X510XXSBDZB4`; there is no source tree identified as `X510XXUCEZE4`.
- The U11 build uses Clang 21.1.8 instead of the recorded Samsung toolchain `clang-r450784d`, and applies patches `0001–0006,0008–0010`; there is no Git commit in the OSRC archive.
- The build log itself declares that it is a compatibility build, not a byte-for-byte reproduction, and that LTO linking is not proven reproducible.
- The previous U3→U11 comparison shows 228 DTS changed, 3,721 drivers changed, 79 new drivers, and 17 new DTS/dtsi. This is not a U12→U11 comparison.

Inferences:

- It is reasonable to treat the U11 kernel as a separate development candidate from stock EZE4/U12, not as an equivalent rebuild.
- The four DTB differences may originate from source, build configuration, or Samsung post-processing, but the exact cause cannot be assigned without U12 source.
- The primary risk is not solely the version number: it also includes any internal changes between `.180` and `.189`, modversions CRCs, ordering/initialization, and non-comparable vendor ramdisk differences.

## Blocking Unknowns

1. Exact `X510XXUCEZE4`/U12 source and its final patches/configuration.
2. Complete modversions CRC table of stock kernel and each stock module.
3. Exported list/symbols from stock vmlinux and individual stock modules.
4. Packaged boot/init_boot/vendor_boot image generated by U11 and its actual AVB/bootconfig/ramdisk tables policy.
5. Content and calibration of `/vendor/firmware/wifi` plus EFS for SCSC Wi-Fi/BT.
6. M2/M3 physical observation: console, early boot, UFS, USB, applied DT, clocks/regulators, and CFI/modprobe errors.
7. Semantic resolution of external DTB references and validation of the full `.config` diff.

## Operational Conclusion

Overlays and several metadata items are promising, but the binary audit does not establish ABI compatibility between the U11 build and stock EZE4/U12. Maintain the physical gate at **NO-GO** until obtaining EZE4/U12 source, comparable CRC/symbol tables, packaged U11 images, and controlled hardware observation.
