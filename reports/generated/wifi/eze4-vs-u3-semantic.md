## Reading and Limitations

The base DT contains 602 renumberings that the comparator can associate with
paths, but still 37 material differences and 10 unresolved external references;
among them are auxiliary `__symbols__`/`__local_fixups__` nodes typical of
overlay representation, which must be interpreted alongside the final DTB.
The overlay retains 17 material differences. External references are treated
as an opaque sentinel solely to avoid filling the table with false changes;
this incomplete normalization forces the `INCOMPLETE` state and prevents
claiming equivalence. Included DTSIs are not compared and an effective tree is
not reconstructed with `fdtoverlay`; validating the selected hardware requires
running DT or bootloader evidence.
